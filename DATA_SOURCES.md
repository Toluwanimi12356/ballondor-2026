## Verification and scope notes

- Trophy and award counts match the supplied correction dataset and are asserted during development in `data/players.ts`.
- Source URLs are the supplied supporting links. They are presented on each profile with the source publisher's name rather than the raw URL.
- Individual-award records contain name, competition, season/year, source, URL and verified state. The Mahou club/sponsor award is tagged `secondary`.
- No competition-level match/goals/assists breakdown is populated; the application keeps that section in an honest unavailable state.
- Nominee video identity is sourced from the supplied presentation. Video source ranges and output properties are documented separately in [VIDEO_ASSETS.md](VIDEO_ASSETS.md).
